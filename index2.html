Four hands-on labs, all running in your browser. Simulated 2D teaching models only, not for clinical decisions.

1 · Tensor lab: shape, direction, FA and MD

The dots are water molecules. Change λ1 and λ2 and watch the shape and the wandering change, or tap a tissue preset.

2 · Scan angle lab: how the scan direction changes the signal

Rotate the green scan direction (or press Sweep). Scanning along the fibre loses signal; scanning across keeps it. Set the b-value to 0 and the contrast vanishes.

3 · How many scan directions do you need?

Each blue dot is one measured direction. The orange shape is fitted to the dots, and the red line is the fibre direction it finds. Compare with the true direction (green dashed).

4 · Tractography parameter playground: why one picture is never the whole truth

A schematic 2D model: a vertical “corticospinal-like” bundle (brainstem at the bottom, cortex at the top) crossed by a horizontal bundle. Drag on the picture to move the seed. For teaching only, not for clinical decisions.
Tip: press Pin this result, change one slider, and compare the new tract (colour) with the pinned one (grey).
