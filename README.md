## 🩺 ***Why this matters for neurosurgeons***

> *Tractography images look clean and certain, but they are* ***estimates*** *that depend on scan quality and software settings.*

These labs let you ***change the settings yourself*** and watch what happens, so you understand what the picture really means.

- 🔬 **Understand the picture.** See how a ***tensor***, ***FA*** and ***MD*** describe water movement, and how ***fibre direction*** is inferred.
- 📡 **Know why scan quality matters.** Explore the ***b-value***, the ***number of directions*** and ***noise***, which helps when discussing protocols with radiology.
- 🎚️ **See why tracts change.** Move the ***FA threshold***, ***turning angle***, ***seed size*** and ***noise***, then ***pin*** one result and compare.
- ⚠️ **Learn the pitfalls.** ***A missing tract does not prove the fibres are absent.*** Loose settings can make tracts ***leak*** into neighbouring bundles.
- 🎓 **Teach with it.** Residents, fellows and students can explore at their own pace.

- ***No patient data*** *is included in this repository.*
